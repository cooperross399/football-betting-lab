# NFL TD Due List backtest

The rule: 8+ touchdowns scored last regular season, listed on a drought of 3+ games for 12+, 4+ games for 10-11, 5+ games for 8-9. Parameters fixed before any outcome or price was compared (`src/football_betting_lab/td_due_list.py`). Generated by `scripts/run_td_due_list_backtest.py`; data from nflverse under CC-BY-4.0.

## 1. Do due players score more often?
