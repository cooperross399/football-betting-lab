# The null baseline: betting everything, with no model

A sound harness returns roughly the vig here. If betting everything makes money, the fault is in the settlement, the prices or the join, and no result computed on top of it means anything.

| Season | Bets | ROI |
|:-------|-----:|----:|
| 2023 | 94,457 | -5.16% |
| 2024 | 142,244 | -8.32% |
| 2025 | 148,794 | -12.82% |
| **all** | 385,495 | -9.28% |

**The baseline is not flat across seasons**, and that matters more than it looks: a market whose ROI falls by seven points between two seasons may simply be sitting on a baseline that fell by seven points. Raw ROIs compared across seasons without this are comparing two different questions.
